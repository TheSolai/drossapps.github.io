--- ## What it is Raised Letters is a local-first writing environment for fiction authors. You write in chapters. The AI reads your whole manuscript. Then — when you ask for feedback — it already knows your world, your characters, your voice. It doesn't give you generic notes. It gives you editorial feedback shaped by the authors who've mastered the craft you're practicing. This is not a ghost-writer. The AI never writes your prose. It reads it, questions it, and tells you what's working, what's not, and why. Built with: Rust desktop shell, React editor, FastAPI backend, local Ollama AI. Your manuscripts stay on your machine. --- ## The Author Personas Forty-three curated editorial voices. Each one carries a system prompt engineered from that author's actual published style — sentence rhythm, diction, imagery preferences, thematic concerns. A RAG corpus service can ingest their public-domain works (Ulysses, Dubliners, The Great Gatsby) and pull matching passages at inference time, giving the AI actual text to draw on.

--- ## What it does

Chapter-based editor

Write in chapters, not one giant file. Named checkpoints for every chapter — save points you can return to, compare, restore.

Whole-manuscript AI context

Before the AI comments on chapter five, it's already read chapters one through six. Its feedback accounts for your entire book — not just the page in front of you.

Editorial feedback modes

Feedback mode (inline commentary), copy edit mode (grammar/punctuation), format mode (manuscript submission checks), revision suggestions (repetitive words, passive voice, telling vs showing).

Companion documents

World Bible (characters, settings, timeline), Style Guide (your preferred rules), Editorial Letter (notes to the AI about what to watch for in your specific book).

Persona-powered editorial voice

Apply an author persona to shape the AI's editorial lens. Get feedback as if Stephen King's editor was reading your draft — or Toni Morrison's, or your own self-critic.

100% local, no cloud required

Runs entirely on your Mac. Ollama handles AI inference locally. Your manuscripts never touch a server. Optional encrypted cloud sync via your own email mailbox.

--- ## How it works

The flow

1

Write

Chapter by chapter. Save checkpoints. Build your world bible and style guide as you go.

2

Select a persona

Choose the editorial voice — James Joyce, a blunt editor, a beta reader, your own self-critic.

3

Ask for feedback

Select a passage. The AI reads your whole manuscript first, then responds in that persona's voice.

4

Revise

The feedback is advisory. You write. The AI observes. The final prose is always yours.
