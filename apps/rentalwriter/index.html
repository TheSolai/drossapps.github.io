--- ## What it is RentalWriter is a stripped-down chapter editor with 5 AI editorial personas. No RAG. No World Bible. No corpus ingestion. No companion documents. You write in chapters. You pick a persona. You get feedback. If you want 43 author personas, RAG corpus ingestion, World Bible, Style Guide, and Editorial Letters — see [iWriterRight](/apps/iwriteright/). --- ## The 5 Personas 5 built-in editorial voices. Read-only. No custom persona creation.

Blunt Editor

Editorial · Modern · Craft

Direct, no-nonsense. Names problems precisely. Quotes the prose. No hedging.

No hedging Quote the line Name the problem

Beta Reader

Editorial · Modern · Reader reaction

Emotional, character-focused. "I didn't buy their motivation." Speaks in first person.

Reads as a person Flags immersion breaks Names what felt unearned

Self-Revision

Editorial · Modern · Inner critic

Your own ruthless inner critic. "Is this what you meant, or did you settle?"

Internal critic Pushes for precision Assumes best version

Plot Doctor

Editorial · Modern · Story structure

Diagnoses act breaks, cause-and-effect, turning points. Scene-by-scene structural review.

Three-act diagnosis Cause-and-effect chain Inciting incident check

Dialogue Doctor

Editorial · Modern · Voice & speech

Does each character sound like themselves? Checks subtext, voice differentiation, exposition dump.

Differentiates voices Subtext over text Catches dialogue-y dialogue

--- ## Features

Chapter-based editor

Write in chapters, not one giant file. Named checkpoints for every chapter — save points you can return to and compare.

5 AI editorial personas

Blunt Editor, Beta Reader, Self-Revision, Plot Doctor, Dialogue Doctor. No configuration required.

AI feedback modes

Feedback (editorial commentary), Copy Edit (grammar/punctuation), Revision (passive voice, repetition), Auto-Fix (polish).

100% local

Runs entirely on your Mac. Ollama handles AI inference locally. Your manuscripts never leave your machine.

No complexity

No World Bible to maintain. No corpus to ingest. No Style Guide to write. Just write and get feedback.

Import / Export

Import: plain text, Markdown, DOCX. Export: plain text, Markdown, DOCX.

--- ## Requirements

📋 Before you buy — read this

RentalWriter requires Ollama (free, open-source) to be installed and running on your Mac. You'll also need to download at least one AI model through Ollama — qwen3.5:35b is recommended. Model downloads are free and happen inside the app. No API keys. No cloud account. No subscription.
