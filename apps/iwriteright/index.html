--- ## What it is iWriterRight is a chapter-based manuscript editor with an AI editorial companion. You write in chapters. The AI reads your whole manuscript before responding. Then — when you ask for feedback — it already knows your world, your characters, your voice. This is not a ghost-writer. The AI never writes your prose. It reads it, questions it, and tells you what's working, what's not, and why. Runs entirely on your Mac. Ollama handles AI inference locally. Your manuscripts never leave your machine. --- ## The Editorial Personas 43 built-in voices, each engineered from a published author's actual style — sentence rhythm, diction, imagery preferences, thematic concerns.

--- ## Features

Chapter-based editor

Write in chapters, not one giant file. Named checkpoints for every chapter — save points you can return to and compare.

Whole-manuscript AI context

Before the AI comments on chapter five, it's already read chapters one through six. Its feedback accounts for your entire book — not just the page in front of you.

Editorial feedback modes

Feedback (inline commentary), Copy Edit (grammar/punctuation), Format (manuscript submission checks), Revision (repetitive words, passive voice, telling vs showing).

Companion documents

World Bible (characters, settings, timeline), Style Guide (your preferred rules), Editorial Letter (notes to the AI about what to watch for).

43 author personas

Apply an editorial voice shaped by real authors. Get feedback as if Stephen King's editor was reading — or Toni Morrison's, or James Joyce's.

100% local

Runs entirely on your Mac. Ollama handles AI inference locally. Your manuscripts never touch a server.

--- ## How it works

The flow

1

Write

Chapter by chapter. Save checkpoints. Build your world bible and style guide as you go.

2

Choose a persona

Pick the editorial voice — James Joyce, a blunt editor, a beta reader, your own self-critic.

3

Ask for feedback

Select a passage. The AI reads your whole manuscript first, then responds in that voice.

4

Revise

The feedback is advisory. You write. The AI observes. The final prose is always yours.

--- ## Requirements

📋 Before you buy — read this

iWriterRight requires Ollama (free, open-source) to be installed and running on your Mac. You'll also need to download at least one AI model through Ollama — qwen3.5:35b is recommended for best results with long manuscripts. Model downloads are free and happen inside the app. No API keys. No cloud account. No subscription.
